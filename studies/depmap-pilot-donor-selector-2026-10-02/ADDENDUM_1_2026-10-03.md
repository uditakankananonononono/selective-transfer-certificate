Findings that make the freeze text inoperable:
1. The library has NO non-targeting guides. 100 genes x 2 guides (v1_g1, v2_g1); the 10 olfactory receptor genes (OR*) are the authors'
   negative controls (their sceptre_benchmark.R maps names starting "OR" to non-targeting).
2. Guide-count matrices cover all 132,888 droplets of C4I, versus 20,682 cells in the filtered expression matrix; per-cell assignment is not pre-labeled.
3. GE files are 10x HDF5; barcodes end "-1", guide matrix barcodes do not.

Clarified rules (replace the ambiguous freeze lines; everything else unchanged):
- Cells = barcodes present in both the filtered GE matrix (after stripping "-1") and the guide matrix.
- Guide assignment follows the authors' released sceptre_benchmark.R: a cell is assigned to its top guide only if that guide holds >=80% of the
  cell's guide UMIs and has >=5 UMIs; otherwise unassigned and dropped. Cell is a KO cell for the gene of its assigned guide (either guide v1/v2 pooled).
- Controls = cells assigned to any OR-gene guide (the 10 ORs pooled), per line. (Freeze said "non-targeting".) The authors' same-chromosome-arm
  control restriction and arm-loss cell removal are NOT applied (simplification, reported as a limitation).
- Cell QC: mitochondrial fraction < 0.25 and total UMIs within the line's 1st-99th percentile (the authors' released thresholds).
- Duplicate gene symbols in the GE features: counts summed per symbol.
- Eligibility thresholds unchanged: >=20 KO cells, >=100 control cells per line.
- OR genes are excluded as KO units (they are controls). Units = the 90 non-OR genes.
- Gene set: operative rule is the top 2000 genes by pooled mean control expression among genes present in all 16 lines (control cells only).
  Pooled mean = unweighted mean of per-line control pseudobulk means of log1p CP10K.
- Everything else (donors, leave-batch-out, M/B/Rnd, certificate, clauses A and B, h2h sealed) as frozen.
