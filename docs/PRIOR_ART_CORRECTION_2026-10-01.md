# Prior-art correction, October 1, 2026

The frozen v1.0 novelty pass was incomplete. It is retained as historical evidence,
not endorsed as a current claim. Confidence-guided filtering of single-cell
perturbation predictions is NOT an invention claim for this project.

PRESCRIBE, section 4.7, evaluates uncertainty-guided removal of low-confidence
predictions and reports improved prediction accuracy after filtering. Its main
setting is unseen gene perturbations, not this experiment's unseen-context
same-perturbation transfer. This difference does not justify claiming novelty
for confidence filtering itself. The exact training-concordance statistic and
prospective donor-transfer selection-value experiment still need a stronger
prior-art pass; absence of identical wording is not novelty proof.

Source independently fetched: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07964

GPerturb supplies Bayesian uncertainty for gene-level perturbation effects.
Uncertainty on effects is established prior art, not a new contribution here.

Source independently fetched: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61165-7

The current frozen Tier-1 result is a LOSS, not a novel-method win: 8/11
full-coverage clauses pass, 3 fail; 28.2497974% retained macro risk reduction
passes the other clause. It also does not isolate selector value because the
comparison is to a full-coverage published baseline. Neither a numerical partial
success nor an uncertainty score establishes novelty. A follow-up requires
strong matched-predictor, matched-coverage comparators and untouched independent
donors before a confirmatory selector-value claim.
